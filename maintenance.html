05

Common questions

Straight answers before you reach out.

Can you take over an app or website someone else built?

Yes. Emmanuel starts with a review of the codebase, reports its state and risks, agrees a plan, and then stabilises and extends it.

Can you fix bugs and crashes in my app?

Yes. He traces crashes and regressions to their root cause rather than patching symptoms, and adds tests so they do not return.

Can you speed up a slow app or website?

Yes. At Barsmiko Inc, code splitting, lazy loading and bundle optimisation cut load times by 40%.

Can you upgrade an old React Native, Flutter or Next.js project?

Yes. Upgrading framework versions and dependencies, and keeping apps compliant with current App Store and Google Play requirements, is part of the work.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes, as an hourly or monthly retainer for fixes, new features and store updates.
