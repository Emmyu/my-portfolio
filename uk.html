Common questions

Straight answers before you reach out.

Can you work with UK businesses and startups?

Yes. Emmanuel works remotely with clients in the UK on fixed-price projects, retainers, fractional CTO engagements and full-time remote roles. He previously built a Web3 social platform with the London-based team at Maringo.

Do you work UK hours?

Yes. Emmanuel overlaps with UK business hours for calls, standups and reviews.

Can you take Stripe payments in pounds?

Yes. Emmanuel integrates Stripe for card payments, which supports GBP among other currencies, and can add wallets, subscriptions and KYC flows.

What do you build for UK startups?

Mobile apps (Flutter, React Native), websites and web apps (Next.js, React), and the Node.js and AWS backends behind them. See the MVP and fractional CTO page.

Is the code mine, and is data handled safely?

You own the delivered code. Emmanuel builds with secure authentication and careful personal-data handling, and works with your advisers on UK GDPR requirements rather than promising compliance alone.

How do I get a quote and get started?

Book a 30-minute call on Calendly or email aderemiemmy204@gmail.com and describe your project: what you want built, for whom, and any deadline. Cost depends on scope, so it is discussed on that call.
