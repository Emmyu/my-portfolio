05

Common questions

Straight answers before you reach out.

Can you build my app for both iPhone and Android?

Yes. Using Flutter or React Native, one codebase produces both the iOS and the Android app, which keeps cost and maintenance lower than two separate native apps. When a feature needs it, native Swift or Kotlin code is added to the same project.

Should my app use Flutter or React Native?

Both give native-grade performance for most products. React Native (TypeScript) suits teams that also have a React web product and want to share logic; Flutter (Dart) suits apps with a highly custom interface. Emmanuel works in both and recommends one after hearing what you are building.

Do you publish the app to the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. That includes App Store Connect and Play Console setup, TestFlight and internal test tracks, store listings, and handling the review process until the app is live.

What kinds of mobile apps have you built?

Fintech and payments (SekiApp digital wallet, Mylo buy-now-pay-later, Papaymoni payments, DuPay POS), healthcare (EveryDose medication reminders), and travel safety (Sitata). All are listed above with their store links.

Can you add payments, wallets or identity verification?

Yes. Emmanuel has integrated Stripe, Paystack and Flutterwave, built digital wallet flows, and implemented biometric authentication and KYC/AML verification, including as CTO of the fintech platform Transmoni.
