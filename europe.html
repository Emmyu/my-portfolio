05

Common questions

Straight answers before you reach out.

Can you work with European businesses and startups?

Yes. Emmanuel works remotely with clients across Europe on fixed-price projects, retainers, fractional CTO engagements and full-time remote roles.

Have you built websites for European brands?

Yes. Examples include Nira Alpina (Swiss alpine resort), Casa Angelina (Amalfi Coast hotel), Moooi (Dutch design brand) and MindDoc (German mental health platform).

Do you work European hours?

Yes. Emmanuel overlaps with UK and Central European business hours for calls and reviews.

Can you build GDPR-aware sites and apps?

Emmanuel builds with consent, secure authentication and careful personal-data handling in mind. Formal GDPR compliance also depends on your legal basis, hosting and processes, so he works with your data protection adviser rather than promising it alone.

Can you build multilingual sites?

Yes. Next.js supports localised routes and translated content, and Emmanuel can set up multilingual structure when a site needs several languages.

How do I get a quote and get started?

Book a 30-minute call on Calendly or email aderemiemmy204@gmail.com and describe your project: what you want built, for whom, and any deadline. Cost depends on scope, so it is discussed on that call.
