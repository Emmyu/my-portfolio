Common questions

Straight answers before you reach out.

Can you build a fintech or digital wallet app?

Yes. Emmanuel has shipped a digital wallet (SekiApp), mobile payments (Papaymoni), a retail POS (DuPay POS) and a buy-now-pay-later app (Mylo), and was CTO of the fintech platform Transmoni, which grew to 10,000+ users.

Which payment gateways can you integrate?

Stripe, Paystack and Flutterwave, covering card, bank transfer and mobile payments for global and African markets.

Can you build KYC and identity verification?

Yes. Emmanuel has implemented KYC/AML verification flows and biometric authentication in production fintech apps.

Who is responsible for financial compliance and licensing?

Emmanuel builds the technical side: secure payment flows, identity verification and safe data handling. Licensing, regulatory approval and legal sign-off stay with your business and compliance advisers, and he works alongside them.

Can you build the backend as well as the app?

Yes. Emmanuel builds the Node.js and AWS serverless backends (Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, S3) as well as the mobile and web clients, so one engineer owns the whole payment flow.

How do I get a quote and get started?

Book a 30-minute call on Calendly or email aderemiemmy204@gmail.com and describe your fintech product: the users, the payment flows and the markets. Cost depends on scope, so it is discussed on that call.
