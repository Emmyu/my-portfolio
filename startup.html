05

Common questions

Straight answers before you reach out.

Can you build my MVP from scratch?

Yes. Emmanuel takes a product from idea to release: scoping, design handoff, mobile and web development, backend, and launch to the App Store, Google Play or the web.

Can you act as my CTO or technical lead?

Yes. Emmanuel was CTO and lead developer of Transmoni, a fintech platform that grew to 10,000+ users, and works as a fractional CTO or tech lead for startups, part-time and remote.

What stack do you recommend for an MVP?

For mobile, React Native (TypeScript) or Flutter (Dart) so one codebase serves iOS and Android; for web, Next.js; for backend, Node.js with Supabase, Firebase or AWS serverless. The final choice depends on your product.

What if I only have an idea and no designs?

That is a normal starting point. The first call is for scoping what the MVP must do; Emmanuel can work from your designs or from wireframes agreed during planning.

Will I own the code?

Yes. The goal is a clean, documented codebase handed over to you, so you are never locked in.

How do I get a quote and get started?

Book a 30-minute call on Calendly or email aderemiemmy204@gmail.com and describe your startup idea: the problem, the users and what the first version must do. Cost depends on scope, so it is discussed on that call.
